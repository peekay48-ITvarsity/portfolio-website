DISCOVER THE PLANETS

Planet Explorer

☿ Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in our solar system.

♀ Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and has a thick atmosphere.

🌍 Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet with life.

♂ Mars

Mars is known as the Red Planet because of the iron minerals in its surface.

♃ Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system and is a gas giant.

♄ Saturn

Saturn is a gas giant famous for its large and beautiful ring system.

♅ Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant with a blue-green appearance and a tilted rotation.

♆ Neptune

Neptune is the eighth planet from the Sun and is known for its strong winds.
